Predicting Weekly Walmart Sales

Can pretrained AI forecast Walmart demand without ever training on it?
Team project for Advanced Deep Learning + ML at UT McCombs.

In the works

12,196product-store series
13%less error than a 4-week average, zero-shot
60%of days the median product sells nothing

Problem

Most products sell nothing on most days.
A forecast can score well and still leave shelves empty.

What I did

  • Prepped 5 years of sales for Walmart's 4 California stores
  • Built the baselines every model is scored against
  • Researched the M5 winning methods and assembled the team blog

Why it matters

Low error isn't enough.
Inventory needs forecasts that add up to real demand.
